Technologies Developed

Each has reached Technology Readiness Level 4: validated in the laboratory.

Low-cost shape-memory characterization system

Measures shape fixity and recovery of 4D-printed parts. Benchmarked against commercial systems and used in laboratory studies behind his publications.

TRL 4 of 9Lab validated

4D-printed mechanical steganography & encryption–decryption device

A laboratory prototype developed in consultation with the Indian Army that hides and reveals information through programmed shape change. Under laboratory testing.

TRL 4 of 9Under testing

Cargo Hyperloop demonstrator

A fully functional 1:20-scale prototype integrating the core functions of the proposed compressed-air cargo transport system, for CMPDI, Ministry of Coal.

TRL 4 of 9Working demonstrator

Multi-material direct ink writing (DIW) 3D printer

Prints soft materials and biomaterials with in-situ material mixing, heating and cooling. Under laboratory testing.
